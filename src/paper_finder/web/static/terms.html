What this is

Paper Analyser is a personal, non-commercial study tool that indexes Cambridge International (CIE) AS & A Level past exam questions so you can find and revise from them by topic or keyword. It is provided as-is, free of charge, with no guarantee of uptime, accuracy, or continued availability — it may be changed, restricted, or shut down at any time without notice.

Payments and refunds

Paper Analyser is free. There is no paid tier, no subscription, no in-app purchase, and no fee of any kind, and there never has been. Because no charge can ever arise, there is nothing to refund.

Accuracy — please read this

The topic label on a question is best-effort, not guaranteed. Some subjects were labelled question-by-question by hand; others (the multiple-choice papers in a few subjects) were labelled by an automated heuristic that is typically only about 80–85% accurate. A small number of questions have no published mark-scheme answer at all, because the original exam board document was missing or incomplete for that question. Don't treat any single result as authoritative — cross-check against the original paper when it matters.
