Privacy policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Who this is for

Paper Analyser is an independent, non-commercial personal study project. It is not a business, sells nothing, and collects no payment details. This page explains, plainly, what happens to your data when you use it.

What we collect

Signing in only needs an email address. You type it in, Supabase (our authentication provider) emails you a one-time 6-digit code, and you type that back. That's it — no name, no profile, no payment details, and nothing else is asked for anywhere in the product.

Because sign-up is open to any address that can receive a code, that first sign-in also creates an account for that address automatically.

What we don't collect

This application does not log, store, or ever look at what you search for. There is no query-history table anywhere in its database. Your recent searches live only in your own browser's local storage, are never sent anywhere but back to you, and you can clear them at any time from the "✕ Clear recent searches" row on the search page.

We don't run analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind — see the cookie policy for the full list of what your browser actually stores.

What we're honest can't be avoided

When you search while signed in, your browser sends the search text straight to our database provider, Supabase, to run the query — we never see or store it ourselves, but Supabase's own infrastructure logs may briefly retain request-level data (including an IP address) as an ordinary operational side effect of running the service, not something this project configures or reads. The same is true of our hosting provider, Vercel, which keeps standard web-server access logs (IP address, browser type, page requested). Neither log is used for anything beyond keeping the service running.

How long we keep it

Your account record is kept until you delete it (see below). Sign-in sessions don't expire automatically, so once you're signed in on a device you generally stay signed in — use "Sign out" if you'd rather not.

Your choices

You can delete your account and everything tied to it, permanently and immediately, from the "Delete account" link next to your email once you're signed in — no need to contact anyone. Signing out or clearing your browser's site data removes everything stored locally.

Children and young people

Paper Analyser is intended for exam candidates roughly 16 and older. If you're younger than that — or younger than 18 where local law treats under-18s as children needing a guardian's involvement, such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act — please use it only with a parent or guardian aware of it. It is not directed at, and should not be used by, children under 13.

The sign-in email

The only email this service sends is the one-time sign-in code, sent because you asked for one. There's no newsletter and no marketing list to unsubscribe from — see the terms of service for more on that.
