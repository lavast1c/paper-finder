Cookie policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Paper Analyser doesn't set any cookies of its own, and uses no tracking, analytics, or advertising technology at all — there is nothing here to track you with in the first place. What it does use is your browser's own localStorage, entirely to remember things you've chosen so the site doesn't reset itself every time you open it. Everything below stays on your device; none of it is sent to us.

What we don't have

  • No tracking cookies.
  • No analytics (no Google Analytics, no Plausible, nothing).
  • No advertising or cross-site identifiers.
  • No third-party cookies set by this site.

You can clear any or all of this at any time from your browser's own site-data settings; doing so simply resets your preferences and signs you out. See the privacy policy for what happens on our side when you search or sign in.
