Accessibility statement

Last updated 18 October 2026

Target

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. This is a personal project maintained in spare time, so treat this page as an honest snapshot rather than a formal conformance claim.

What's in place

  • The public subject, topic and past-paper pages are plain HTML text with a clear heading structure and breadcrumbs, readable with any screen reader and without JavaScript.
  • Light and dark themes, both checked against WCAG AA text contrast; the theme toggle is a labelled button.
  • The curriculum and subject dropdowns are labelled controls.
  • Every question image has a "Show text" alternative with the extracted wording.
  • Reduced-motion and reduced-transparency settings are honoured (see below).

Known limitations

  • The question/mark-scheme crops are pictures of the original exam page — a screen reader cannot read a diagram inside one. The "Show text" toggle next to each crop is the real text alternative; use it if you rely on a screen reader or need to copy the wording.
  • The frosted-glass panels are semi-transparent over a live animated background, so a measured contrast ratio is a best case rather than a guarantee at every instant. Anyone who sets prefers-reduced-transparency or whose browser lacks backdrop-filter gets solid, opaque panels instead.
  • prefers-contrast and Windows "forced colors" mode are not specifically handled yet.

Feedback

If something here doesn't work with your assistive technology, please email vihaantalluriwork@gmail.com with what you tried and what happened. This is a one-person project, so replies may take a while, but every report is read.
