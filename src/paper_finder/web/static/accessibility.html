Accessibility statement

Last updated 18 September 2026

Target

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. This is a personal project maintained in spare time, so treat this page as an honest snapshot rather than a formal conformance claim.

What's been fixed

  • The sign-in and search inputs now have real, programmatically-associated labels, not just placeholder text.
  • A "Skip to content" link (appears on keyboard focus) lets you jump past the header.
  • The header no longer stays keyboard-focusable while faded out by scroll.
  • The paper/year/season/topic filter dropdowns and the curriculum/subject dropdowns support arrow-key navigation, not just Tab.
  • The question and mark-scheme image viewers can be focused and operated from the keyboard (arrow keys to pan, +/−/0 to zoom/reset), not only with a mouse or touch.
  • The crop resize handles are real, focusable buttons with a keyboard equivalent (arrow keys) to dragging.
  • Image alt text now names what the image is and points at the "Show text" alternative, instead of a bare "image 1 of 3".
  • The single-key shortcuts below (arrow keys, space, f, +/−/0) only act while the flashcard has focus, so they don't collide with normal typing or a screen reader's own keys elsewhere on the page.
  • The focus outline and the checked-checkbox colour were adjusted in light mode where they measured below the 3:1 contrast a visible focus indicator needs — nothing else in the colour palette changed.

Known limitations

  • The question/mark-scheme crops are pictures of the original exam page — a screen reader cannot read a diagram inside one. The "Show text" toggle next to each crop is the real text alternative; use it if you rely on a screen reader or need to copy the wording.
  • The frosted-glass panels are semi-transparent over a live animated background, so a measured contrast ratio is a best case rather than a guarantee at every instant. Anyone who sets prefers-reduced-transparency or whose browser lacks backdrop-filter gets solid, opaque panels instead.
  • prefers-contrast and Windows "forced colors" mode are not specifically handled yet.

Feedback

If something here doesn't work with your assistive technology, the most useful thing you can do is delete your account and stop using the service if it's not usable for you — there is no support inbox behind this project, and we'd rather say that plainly than promise a response we can't deliver.
